# Interactive video: all 32 directory entries investigated

November 26, 2026

## Main finding

The directory mixes several different markets: interactive lessons, branching stories, business video overlays, shoppable video and enterprise hosting. Compare tools within their intended use case before trying to rank them together.

This public-source pass now covers all 32 directory entries, plus Lumi as an additional starting option. Every remaining vendor URL was attempted; inaccessible or ambiguous offerings remain explicitly unresolved. Evidence comes from public vendor pages, official documentation, acquisition announcements and, for Viewbix, a secondary reproduction of a company filing. No accounts or editors were tested; a live product page does not establish commercial availability, support or quality.

## Additional option missing from the directory

[Lumi](https://lumi.education/en/) provides desktop and cloud H5P authoring options. Its site advertises HTML/SCORM export and website embedding. It is an H5P authoring/deployment route, not a separate interaction format. This makes **H5P plus Lumi** a useful portability-focused baseline to investigate.

## What changed after checking the other 18

**Additional candidates worth a closer look:** Ivory for broad interactive authoring; VidVersity and Kumullus for workplace training; Verse for narratives; Spott and Wootag for commerce/advertising. ClickView and Nearpod are particularly relevant when the goal is classroom learning.

**Directory entries needing caution:** DilogR's link lands on domain parking; Exaltive's availability remains unresolved; Viewbix has inaccessible domains and evidence of a changed business focus. Klynt publishes an installation page but needs a maintenance/compatibility check. Viddler's current checked pages establish hosting, not interactive authoring.

**Do not equate specialization with shutdown:** Luma1, PlayFilm and Vudoo describe narrower workflow or commerce propositions. Wootag has a new homepage emphasis while still publishing a video-product page. Spott and Verse both retain substantive public offerings.

## Provisional starting comparison

Start with **H5P plus Lumi**, **Stornaway**, and **Mindstamp, hihaho or Ivory** to compare portable learning content, branching experiences and general-purpose overlays/reporting. These are representative starting tracks, not a quality ranking.

For focused comparisons, use VidVersity/Kumullus for workplace training, ClickView/Nearpod for classrooms, Verse for narratives, and Smartzer/Spott/Vudoo/Wootag for commerce. Brightcove/Blue Billywig remain relevant when enterprise hosting and services matter.

The next useful step is to choose the intended interaction/use case, then inspect pricing, export/lock-in, mobile accessibility and actual branching behavior for three comparable candidates. There is not yet enough evidence to name a winner or claim one tool is cheaper.

## Source limitations

The [original hihaho directory](https://hihaho.com/blog/interactive-video-software-tools-platforms/) is vendor-authored, displays a 2024 publication date and uses May 2023 comparison metrics. Original vendor links were extracted from that directory rather than inferred from product names.

Direct access worked for most remaining sites. The Chrome DevTools connector could not launch because its shared browser profile was already in use; that browser was not stopped or reconfigured. The app's interactive browser was used instead and confirmed DilogR's redirect to a parked page. Exaltive and Viewbix browser reads timed out, so browser navigation requests did not establish successful access to those services. No login, signup, contact, purchase or download was submitted.

Two earlier deep links returned 404 (one Vimeo overview and one WeVideo product URL); alternate official Vimeo documentation and the PlayPosit migration notice supplied the evidence above. Klynt's /download/ path redirects to a moved-page notice, but its linked installation page remains readable. Broken links, old-looking pages and transport failures are not by themselves evidence of shutdown.
